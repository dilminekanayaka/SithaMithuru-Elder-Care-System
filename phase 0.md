# Phase 0.1 – Product Foundation

## Project

**Name:** SithaMithuru – Smart SOS and Well-Being Medication Assistant

---

## Purpose

Create a production-ready healthcare mobile platform for elderly users and guardians. The system prioritizes safety, medication adherence, emergency response, and offline-first operation.

---

## Product Vision

Enable elderly people to live independently while giving guardians confidence through reliable monitoring, timely alerts, and simple healthcare management.

---

## Mission

Build an accessible, secure, offline-capable healthcare application that combines emergency assistance, medication management, daily task support, mood journaling, and guardian communication.

---

## Primary Users

### Elder

- Manages medication
- Completes daily tasks
- Uses SOS voice detection
- Records mood
- Receives reminders

### Guardian

- Monitors elder health
- Reviews medication adherence
- Responds to emergencies
- Receives notifications
- Reviews reports

---

## Business Goals

1. Improve medication adherence.
2. Reduce emergency response time.
3. Support independent living.
4. Reduce caregiver anxiety.
5. Provide reliable operation without continuous internet.

---

## Core Value Proposition

- Offline-first
- Privacy-preserving on-device AI
- Guardian monitoring
- Simple elderly-friendly UI
- Single integrated healthcare platform

---

## Functional Scope

- Authentication
- Elder/Guardian pairing
- Medication management

- Mood journal

- Offline synchronization

---

## Non-Functional Goals

- Performance
- Security

- Scalability

---

## User Personas

### Elder

- Age: 60+
- Limited technical knowledge
- Needs large fonts and simple navigation
- Requires reminders and emergency support

### Guardian

- Age: 25–60
- Checks app briefly several times daily
- Wants quick insight and actionable alerts

---

## Success Metrics

- Medication adherence rate
- Emergency notification delivery time
- Daily active users
- Offline synchronization success rate
- Task completion rate
- User satisfaction

---

## Product Principles

- Safety first
- Simplicity over complexity
- Accessibility by default
- Offline before online

- Fast response
- Consistent UI
